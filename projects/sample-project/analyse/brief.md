# Problem Brief

## Problem Statement
Small teams waste hours each week context-switching between tools to track tasks, blockers, and progress.

## Target User
Product teams of 2–10 people who want lightweight coordination without enterprise overhead.

## Key Hypothesis
A focused task tool with minimal setup will outperform heavyweight PM suites for small teams.

## Success Criteria
- Team can onboard in under 5 minutes
- No training required
- Works on mobile and desktop
